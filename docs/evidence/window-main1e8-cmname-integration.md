recovered name can denote different addresses in the two source contexts.
The compiler's low-halfword SDA21 record position is compared at its instruction

or nonallocated debug identity is not claimed for the typed-call migration.

This supersedes the historical incomplete-CMName-provider and eight-SDA-target
qualification for this combined source. It does not establish every surrounding
