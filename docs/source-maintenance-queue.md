# Source-maintenance queue policy

`source-maintenance` is an operator-selected batch policy for independently reviewed C/C++ correctness and readability changes that need not increase matching coverage. It is distinct from tools-only `maintenance`: every selected PR must have kind `source-maintenance`, and the combined diff is limited to C/C++ files under `src/` or `include/`. The manifest, exact heads, review evidence and environment are selected and pinned by the queue operator, not by candidate code. Recovery retains its positive-gain requirement.

This policy still needs every recovery check, the original DOL hash, nonnegative reported deltas, and the external zero-loss guard. The guard checks unfiltered original-address code, data and function coverage, exactness and source-link ranges, rejects all reported loss categories including padding, binds raw reports to the reviewed roots, and reconciles measures with the receipt. It requires identical actual ordered linker input fingerprints for this policy. A reported gain is accepted only when no earlier range is lost. Malformed or missing evidence rejects. It does not change `report_delta`'s padding evidence rules.

## Rollout, after independent review

The acceptance adapter and guard in this change are proposals, not installed trusted tools. Review their exact source, tests and operator contract independently before installing pinned copies outside candidate roots. Pin the adapter, base validator, wrapper, graph review, environment and guard; retain isolated imports and the existing queue's live readiness, review, head freeze, CAS and durable journal checks. The guard must be the external validator for source-maintenance; the legacy validator alone cannot satisfy its receipt contract.

For a pilot, the coordinator selects a frozen reviewed source-maintenance batch and current main, plans a candidate, reserves disjoint private roots, prepares them under the established exclusive protocol, and obtains independent review of each actual graph, launchers and immutable inputs. Only after that review should the sole executor run fresh validation and publication. Do not regenerate or download inputs in the frozen graph, repurpose existing preparation, or reuse results after target/head/tool/review changes. If the target moves, replan and review applicability and graphs again. This proposal does not authorize a pilot, deployment, or publication.
